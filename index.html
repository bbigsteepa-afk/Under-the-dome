About the movie

A father's fight against impossible odds.

John Q follows John Quincy Archibald, whose young son urgently needs a heart transplant. When insurance will not cover the operation and time is running out, John is pushed into a desperate confrontation at the hospital.

DIRECTOR Nick Cassavetes
WRITER James Kearns
STARRING Denzel Washington
ALSO STARRING Robert Duvall, James Woods
“A gripping story about family, sacrifice, and how far a parent will go for their child.”
